Brixo Studio is where you make games. It's free, it runs on Windows and Mac, and it comes with Brixo Player, which is what people use to play.

## Download

1. Go to [playbrixo.com/download](/download) (you'll need to be logged in).
2. Under **Brixo Studio**, press **Download**.
3. Open the file you downloaded.

**On Windows**, you may see *"Windows protected your PC"*. Brixo is new and doesn't have a paid publisher certificate yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Studio installs itself, adds a Start Menu shortcut and opens. No admin needed.

**On a Mac**, open the `.dmg`, drag **Brixo Studio** onto **Applications**, and open it from there. The first time, your Mac says it can't check the app: click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens normally.

Get **Brixo Player** the same way, so you can play games from the website.

## Updating

Studio checks for a new version each time it starts. When there is one, it tells you: download it from the website the same way, and it replaces the old one. Your games aren't touched.

## Where your games are kept

**File > Save** in Studio keeps your game as a `.brixo` file. It suggests the **My Games** folder inside your **Brixo** folder (`C:\Users\<you>\Brixo\My Games` on Windows, `~/Brixo/My Games` on a Mac), but you can put it anywhere, and keep as many games as you like. **File > Open** opens one again. Publishing to the website also keeps a copy there, online. See [Publish and play with friends](publishing).

> **Tip:** Made games with an older Studio? They were saved as `scene.brixo` in your Brixo folder. Open it with **File > Open**, then **Save As** to give it a proper name.

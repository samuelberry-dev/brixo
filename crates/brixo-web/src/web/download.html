Installing on Windows

  1. Download Brixo Player and open the file.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Brixo is new and doesn't have a paid publisher certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. That's it: Brixo Player installs itself (no admin needed) and adds a Start Menu shortcut. Now press Play on any game.

To uninstall, go to Windows Settings → Apps and uninstall Brixo Player or Brixo Studio like any other app. Your saved games stay in your Brixo folder.
